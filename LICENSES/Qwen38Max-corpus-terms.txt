WHAT THE FILES ARE

The two directories hold a constitution corpus of 197 advice records and 736
stories, about 2.0 million tokens, written by the model qwen/qwen3.8-max-0902
(Qwen 3.8 Max, the 2026-09-02 snapshot, made by Alibaba Cloud). The model was
called through OpenRouter on 2026-11-12, routed only to its Alibaba provider
(Alibaba Cloud Model Studio).

The records come from the generation pipeline that wrote this repository's
Claude-written constitution corpus, re-run from Claude's constitution (released
under CC0 1.0) with Qwen 3.8 Max in every role, including the scoring that
filters the records. They are machine-generated text, not human-written. The
generator comparison trained adapters on a 1M-token pool drawn from them.
