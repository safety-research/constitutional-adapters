WHAT THE FILE IS

The file holds the 777 jailbreak prompts that the paper's ReNeLLM evaluations
replayed. Each prompt is a machine-generated rewrite of one of 259 public
harmful-behaviour requests: 159 from HarmBench and 100 from JailbreakBench, both
released under the MIT licence. Each rewrite is placed in each of the three
scenario frames of ReNeLLM (Ding et al., "A Wolf in Sheep's Clothing:
Generalized Nested Jailbreak Prompts can Fool Large Language Models Easily",
2024, arXiv:2311.08268; the method's code is released under the MIT licence),
so every behaviour appears three times.

The rewrites were generated by the model x-ai/grok-4.5, called through
OpenRouter, on 2026-08-21. They are machine-generated text, not human-written.
The three scenario frames were added by a fixed template.
